# How-to

Short answers to "how do I...". Things marked **New** arrived in 0.6.0, the
release that added the X4 Pro.

## Install and update

- **Put RetroInk on an X3 or X4.** Download `firmware-x3-x4-v*.bin` from the
  [Releases page](https://github.com/smashedllama/retroink/releases) and use
  the [web flash tool](./installation.html#install-via-the-web-flash-tool-easiest)
  or an [SD card update](./installation.html#install-via-sd-card).
- **Put RetroInk on an X4 Pro. New** Download `firmware-x4-pro-v*.bin` and
  follow [the Pro steps](./installation.html#x4-pro). Back up the stock
  firmware first.
- **Update later.** On an X3 or X4, **Settings > System > Check for Updates**.
  The Pro does the same: each release carries both files and the reader picks
  its own.

## Reading

- **Open the reader menu.** X3/X4: press **Select** in a book. X4 Pro **New**:
  swipe up from the page, or hold the Home key.
- **Turn pages.** X3/X4: the side rocker or front buttons. X4 Pro **New**: tap
  the right side of the page or swipe left; tap the left third or swipe right
  to go back.
- **Highlight a passage on the Pro. New** Hold a word for half a second, drag
  to the end of the passage, and lift. It is saved as a clipping, and sent to
  Obsidian if you have that set up.
- **Look up a word on the Pro. New** Hold the word and let go without
  dragging.
- **Change the font size.** Reader menu > Font. On the Pro you can also pinch.

## The look

- **Change the desktop pattern.** **Settings > Display > Desktop Pattern.**
- **Turn something on or off.** On/off settings are a rocker marked **O | I**.
  The pressed half is the state: **I** pressed is on, **O** pressed is off.
  **New**
- **Change the sleep screen.** **Settings > Display > Sleep Screen >
  Wallpaper.** Browse with a live preview, and pick one.

## Library

- **Move many files at once.** In the web file manager (File Transfer), tick
  the files, press **Move Selected**, and choose the destination once.
- **Switch shelves.** The Up/Down keys on X3/X4. On the Pro, the two edge
  keys, or tap the left or right part of the shelf banner. **New**
- **Open a book's actions.** Hold the book on the Pro, or press **Select**
  on X3/X4.

## Calendar and clippings

- **See your calendar on the reader.** Set up
  [Calendar Sync](./calendar-sync.html), then open **Desk Accessories > Desk
  Calendar**.
- **Open a day with all its events.** Tap the day, then on the Pro tap the
  events listed under the month. **New**
- **Send highlights to Obsidian.** See [Obsidian Clipping
  Sync](./obsidian-sync.html).

## Brightness (X4 Pro) New

- **Open the quick panel.** Swipe down from the top edge.
- **Change brightness or warmth.** Drag the square on the scroll bar, or tap
  an arrow.
- **Turn the light off.** Press the **O** side of the rocker.

## Games and accessories

- **Play Minesweeper (X4 Pro). New** Desk Accessories > Minesweeper. Tap to
  dig, hold to flag.
- **Play the Puzzle.** Desk Accessories > Puzzle. Tap a tile next to the gap.
- **Play Marble Maze (X3).** Desk Accessories > Marble Maze. Tilt the reader.
