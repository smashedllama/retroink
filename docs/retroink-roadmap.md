# RetroInk roadmap

Ideas for what could come next. None of these have dates, and none are
promised. Features that have shipped are listed in the
[changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Possible next

- **X4 Classic support.** It shares the Pro's chip (ESP32-S3) but has buttons
  only. The X4 Pro was added in 0.6.0; the Classic has not been built or
  tested yet.
- **More time on the X4 Pro.** It has had less real-world use than the X3 and
  X4, so expect fixes and polish to keep arriving.
- **More than one calendar in Calendar Sync.** For example work and home,
  possibly marked differently on the Desk Calendar.
- **Scrapbook.** Saved clippings and bookmarks together in one Mac-style desk
  accessory, with book and date filters.
- **Owner card.** An owner or contact line that can show on the sleep screen,
  useful if a reader is lost.
