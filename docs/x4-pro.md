# RetroInk on the X4 Pro

> **New in 0.6.0.** The X4 Pro is the newest device RetroInk runs on. It has
> had less use than the X3 and X4, so expect some rough edges and please
> report them on [GitHub](https://github.com/smashedllama/retroink/issues).

The Pro has no front buttons. RetroInk is drawn for it with a touch-first
layout: the same System 6 windows, with a tappable Back box in every title
bar, direct taps on rows and days, and swipes for paging.

## What you have to work with

- **The touch screen.** Tap, hold and swipe. Two-finger pinch resizes text in a
  book and a two-finger twist rotates the page.
- **The Home key** (capacitive, under the screen). A short press goes Home.
  A long press opens the reader menu while you are reading.
- **Two edge keys.** They move the highlight in lists and turn pages in a book.
  Tap a row to open it; there is no Select key, so tapping is how you choose.
- **Power.** It keeps its own short and long press actions from
  **Settings > Controls**. It no longer doubles as Select.

## Getting around

- **Back** is the box at the left of every title bar. A swipe in from the left
  edge also goes back.
- **The key bar** at the bottom shows only what the screen needs, such as
  **Options**, **Shuffle** or **New**. Many screens have no key bar at all.
- **Lists** open on a tap. Nothing stays highlighted until you press an edge
  key, which brings the highlight back.

## The quick panel

Swipe down from the top edge, or tap the battery and clock corner of any
title bar.

- **Brightness** and **Warmth** are scroll-bar sliders: drag the square, or tap
  the arrows to step.
- The **I | O** rocker turns the light on and off.
- The row of keys is **Home**, **Stats**, **Sync**, **Dark** mode, **Prefs**
  (global settings) and the **Touch** toggle for the reader. **Home** is there
  for anyone who has given the Home key another job. **Sync** offers book
  progress, nearby position sync, sending a book, and **Sync Stats** for
  reading stats between readers.
- Close it with the close box, or by dragging the striped tab at the bottom
  up.

## Reading

- **Tap** the left third of a page to go back and the rest to go forward, or
  **swipe** sideways.
- **Swipe up** from the page to open the reader menu. It is a drawer with five
  tabs: font, layout, more, bookmarks and settings.
- **Hold a word** for about half a second and a highlight starts under your
  finger. **Drag** to extend it and lift to save it as a clipping. If you let
  go without dragging, that word is looked up in the dictionary instead.
- **Pinch** to change the font size, **twist** with two fingers to rotate.

## Other things that work differently

- **Library.** Tap a book to select it, tap it again to open it, hold it for
  its actions. Swipe sideways to page through the shelf and use the edge keys
  to switch shelves.
- **Desk Calendar.** Swipe sideways to change month, tap a day, then tap the
  events listed below to open the Day View.
- **Sleep screen picker.** Swipe or tap the sides to step through the screens
  and tap the middle to use the one on show.
- **Focus Session.** The length and your goal have on-screen step buttons.
- **Date picker.** Tap a field and use its minus and plus buttons; hold to
  repeat.

## Desk Accessories on the Pro

The Pro has a clock chip, so it has the Clock, Moon Phase, Earth and Desk
Calendar without asking for a date, plus the reading-stats sleep screens.

- **Minesweeper** is new and only on touch screens. Tap to dig, press **Flag**
  to make taps plant flags instead, and tap a number whose flags are all
  placed to clear around it. **New Game** is in the **Menu**, so it can't be
  tapped by accident. The first dig is never a mine.
- **Marble Maze** is not on the Pro, because it needs a motion sensor the Pro
  does not have.

## Known limits

- **Updates:** **Check for Updates** finds the Pro file in the same release as
  the X3/X4 one, so once it is installed the Pro updates over Wi-Fi like the
  others. The first install is by hand, as described in
  [Installation](./installation.html#x4-pro).
- A page of dense text is refreshed a screen at a time, as on any e-ink
  reader, so very quick taps can queue up.
- Some screens in the reader still use the reader's own font in the drawer
  panes instead of the System 6 face.
